The ones to watch 🌟

Meet the masters athletes leading the charge to Perth — from a 38-year-old triathlete to a 72-year-old bowler whose birthday falls on finals day.

Headline ambassador🌟

Margaret Ko

🇯🇵

Swimming · Japan · 58 at the Games

“Kansai 2027 was the best two weeks of my life. Perth is where I'll defend my title — and I'm bringing twelve clubmates.”

⚡ Fun fact: Swam her first masters race at 41 — now holds three Japanese national age-group records.

📍 Perth pick: Sunrise laps at Cottesloe, coffee in Fremantle after.

Margaret rediscovered swimming after her kids left home. Within five years she was on the blocks at the World Masters Games. Her club in Osaka now trains specifically for Perth 2029.

See Swimming at Perth 2029 →

Headline ambassador🌟

Dave Okafor

🇬🇧

Track & Field · United Kingdom · 47 at the Games

“Masters athletics gave me back the athlete I thought I'd retired. Perth in October? Perfect sprint weather.”

⚡ Fun fact: Ran 11.2s for 100m at 45 — faster than he ran at 25.

📍 Perth pick: Kings Park at golden hour. I've seen the photos. I need to be in one.

A former county-level sprinter, Dave came back to the track at 40 after a 15-year break. He now anchors his club's 4x100m relay and mentors first-time masters athletes.

See Track & Field at Perth 2029 →

Sport ambassador🌟

Sue Tan

🇸🇬

Badminton · Singapore · 52 at the Games

“In Singapore we say badminton is for life. Perth 2029 is five hours away — our whole league is talking about it.”

⚡ Fun fact: Plays five nights a week and captains three different club teams.

📍 Perth pick: Rottnest Island. A quokka selfie is non-negotiable.

Sue organised a 22-player contingent to the Pan Pacific Masters Games. For Perth she's aiming for fifty.

See Badminton at Perth 2029 →

Sport ambassador🌟

Hannah Richter

🇩🇪

Cycling — Road · Germany · 61 at the Games

“German masters cycling is enormous, but we race the same roads every year. A world title race on the other side of the world? That's why you keep training.”

⚡ Fun fact: Has ridden a gran fondo in 14 countries. Australia will be number 15.

📍 Perth pick: The Swan Valley vineyards — by bike, naturally.

Hannah leads a 60-strong women's cycling collective in Bavaria and is planning a three-week WA trip around the Games — racing first, Margaret River after.

See Cycling — Road at Perth 2029 →

Sport ambassador🌟

Tom Parata

🇳🇿

Rugby · New Zealand · 55 at the Games

“Masters rugby is the only sport where the after-match is a podium event. See you in Perth.”

⚡ Fun fact: Has played in six golden oldies festivals — and won the 'best third half' award at three.

📍 Perth pick: A west-coast sunset over the Indian Ocean. We don't get those at home.

Tom's Wellington side has toured together for twenty years. Perth 2029 is pencilled in as the trip that brings the old squad back together — partners included.

See Rugby at Perth 2029 →

Market ambassador🌟

Grace Mwangi

🇦🇺

Half Marathon · Australia (WA) · 44 at the Games

“30,000 athletes are about to discover what we locals already know — October in Perth is magic.”

⚡ Fun fact: Runs the Perth foreshore every Saturday with a 200-member social running crew.

📍 Perth pick: My home course IS the Games course. I run the bridges loop every week.

Grace founded one of Perth's biggest social running groups. She's leading the local charge: her goal is 500 WA runners on the half marathon start line.

See Half Marathon at Perth 2029 →

Market ambassador🌟

Ken Yamada

🇯🇵

Golf · Japan · 67 at the Games

“They tell me Perth has sandbelt courses and no winter. I have already told my wife we are going.”

⚡ Fun fact: Has played golf on his birthday for 41 consecutive years.

📍 Perth pick: A round at sunrise, fish and chips in Fremantle at sunset.

Ken competed at Kansai 2027 with three lifelong friends. They made a pact at the closing ceremony: next stop, Perth.

See Golf at Perth 2029 →

Market ambassador🌟

Maria Rossi

🇮🇹

Volleyball (Indoor) · Italy · 49 at the Games

“We play for the third set, the team dinner and the stories. Perth gives us the best version of all three.”

⚡ Fun fact: Her club team has played together since high school — 30 years and counting.

📍 Perth pick: Wine tasting in Margaret River. For research purposes.

Maria's Torino-based team went to the 2013 World Masters Games in their home city. Perth 2029 is the reunion tour.

See Volleyball (Indoor) at Perth 2029 →

Market ambassador🌟

Pete Jacobs

🇦🇺

Lawn Bowls · Australia (QLD) · 72 at the Games

“People ask when I'll slow down. I tell them: after Perth.”

⚡ Fun fact: Took up bowls at 60 'temporarily'. Twelve years later he practises daily.

📍 Perth pick: The Games Village. I'm told there's live music every night — I'll be there.

Pete has competed at every Australian Masters Games since 2017. Perth 2029 will be his first world Games — and his 73rd birthday falls on finals day.

See Lawn Bowls at Perth 2029 →

Sport ambassador🌟

Lena Novak

🇺🇸

Triathlon · USA · 38 at the Games

“A world masters tri on a closed course at a regatta centre, then two weeks exploring WA? Booked. Mentally, at least.”

⚡ Fun fact: Qualified for masters racing the day she turned 30 — and hasn't missed a season since.

📍 Perth pick: An open-water swim at Cottesloe before the race even starts.

Lena races with USMS and USA Triathlon. She's building a Perth 2029 training squad from her Denver masters swim lane.
