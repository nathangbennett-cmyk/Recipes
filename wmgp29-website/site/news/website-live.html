Perth 2029 is live: pre-registration opens for the world's biggest masters games

Home › News › Perth 2029 is live: pre-registration ope…

World Masters Games Perth 2029 will be held from 12–23 October 2029, bringing 30,000 athletes from more than 100 nations to Western Australia. It is the world's biggest multi-sport participation event — open to everyone who meets the minimum age for their sport (most from 30, some from 25), with no qualification required.

Pre-registration is free and takes under a minute. Pre-registrants get first access to Early Bird pricing when paid registration opens in October 2028, sport-specific updates as the program is confirmed, and early previews of official travel packages.

Australia has hosted the World Masters Games three times — Brisbane 1994, Melbourne 2002 and Sydney 2009. Perth 2029 is the nation's return to the world stage, four years out from the Brisbane 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games.
