Calling all club captains: bring your whole crew to Perth

Home › News › Calling all club captains: bring your wh…

The best masters trips are club trips. When registration opens in October 2028, clubs registering five or more members get 10% off every registration — and club milestone rewards stack up from there: a club banner at your venue at 10 registrations, a feature on the Perth 2029 channels at 25, your own Games Village marquee session at 50, and a CEO welcome video plus Village naming rights for one social event at 100+.

Team captains get a dedicated dashboard to manage their group, a direct line to the Perth 2029 team, and a priority pack-collection window when they arrive.

Captains: register your interest now and flag your club — we'll set you up before the rush.
