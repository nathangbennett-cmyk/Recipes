Lifesaving (Pool & Surf) essentials

What is the minimum age for Lifesaving (Pool & Surf) at Perth 2029?

The minimum age for Lifesaving (Pool & Surf) is 30. Competition runs in five-year age groups based on your age on 12 October 2029.

Where will Lifesaving (Pool & Surf) be held?

Lifesaving (Pool & Surf) is proposed for Perth HPC / Scarborough Beach in the Coastal Venues cluster. Venues are confirmed progressively ahead of registration opening in October 2028.

How many athletes will compete in Lifesaving (Pool & Surf)?

Planning ranges expect 550–740 Lifesaving (Pool & Surf) athletes at Perth 2029, based on entries at Sydney 2009, Auckland 2017 and Taipei 2025.
