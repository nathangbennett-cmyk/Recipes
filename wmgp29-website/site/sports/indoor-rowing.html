Indoor Rowing essentials

What is the minimum age for Indoor Rowing at Perth 2029?

The minimum age for Indoor Rowing is 30. Competition runs in five-year age groups based on your age on 12 October 2029.

Where will Indoor Rowing be held?

Indoor Rowing is proposed for Perth HPC in the Perth HPC & Arena Precinct cluster. Venues are confirmed progressively ahead of registration opening in October 2028.

How many athletes will compete in Indoor Rowing?

Planning ranges expect 165–275 Indoor Rowing athletes at Perth 2029, based on entries at Sydney 2009, Auckland 2017 and Taipei 2025.
