Archery (Outdoor) essentials

What is the minimum age for Archery (Outdoor) at Perth 2029?

The minimum age for Archery (Outdoor) is 30. Competition runs in five-year age groups based on your age on 12 October 2029.

Where will Archery (Outdoor) be held?

Archery (Outdoor) is proposed for Whiteman Park International Shooting Complex in the Whiteman Park cluster. Venues are confirmed progressively ahead of registration opening in October 2028.

How many athletes will compete in Archery (Outdoor)?

Planning ranges expect 165–220 Archery (Outdoor) athletes at Perth 2029, based on entries at Sydney 2009, Auckland 2017 and Taipei 2025.
