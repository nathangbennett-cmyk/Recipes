Futsal essentials

What is the minimum age for Futsal at Perth 2029?

The minimum age for Futsal is 30. Competition runs in five-year age groups based on your age on 12 October 2029.

Where will Futsal be held?

Futsal is proposed for Pro Futsal Bibra Lakes / Lords Subiaco in the Outer Metro & Specialist cluster. Venues are confirmed progressively ahead of registration opening in October 2028.

How many athletes will compete in Futsal?

Planning ranges expect 385–550 Futsal athletes at Perth 2029, based on entries at Sydney 2009, Auckland 2017 and Taipei 2025.
