Rowing essentials

What is the minimum age for Rowing at Perth 2029?

The minimum age for Rowing is 27. Competition runs in five-year age groups based on your age on 12 October 2029.

Where will Rowing be held?

Rowing is proposed for Champion Lakes in the Champion Lakes & South-East cluster. Venues are confirmed progressively ahead of registration opening in October 2028.

How many athletes will compete in Rowing?

Planning ranges expect 1,020–1,380 Rowing athletes at Perth 2029, based on entries at Sydney 2009, Auckland 2017 and Taipei 2025.
